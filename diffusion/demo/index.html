Swarm shape lab

256 identical bots move from noise or a pile into a target shape. Pick a trained model, pick or draw a shape, press run.

Grey = target shape, red = bots. "Cut a chunk" removes the bots near a random spot of the finished shape; "Repair" restarts the clock and lets the same model refill it (only models trained with damage do). Shapes you draw are centred and scaled to the training range. Models run on CPU on a research box.
